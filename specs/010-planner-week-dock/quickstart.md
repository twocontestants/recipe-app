# Quickstart: Week dock and day tones

```sh
npm test
```

Expected: `plannerDayTone` cases in `lib/plannerDays.test.ts`.

## Manual

1. Open the planner on a phone. Scroll to Sunday — week picker is visible above Planner/Recipes/Shopping.
2. Tap next — next week loads. Tap Today — this week returns.
3. Today’s dinner card has a light rust wash. Yesterday’s card is greyer. Tomorrow’s is normal. Weekday names stay readable.
